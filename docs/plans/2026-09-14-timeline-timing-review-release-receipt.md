# Timeline timing review

The [timeline Analyst view](https://researchtools.net/dashboard/tools/timeline) now offers **Timing review** when relative before/after placements exist. It compares each event's recorded calendar bounds with its anchor, shows agreement, disagreement or unresolved results, and links directly to both events. The transient check updates after edits or imports.

Unknown, overlapping or ambiguous inputs are not reported as agreement. Recorded year/month/day and minute/second precision is retained. This checks analyst placement consistency, not independent evidence or actual timing; it does not change events, selection, extraction, backups or saved revisions. **TL-06 is in progress:** structured intervals, circa dates, clock mappings and extraction v2 remain pending. TL-17 remains partial and TL-13 planning is not delivered.
