# Timeline presentation scheduling

[Timeline](https://researchtools.net/dashboard/tools/timeline) now offers **Use presentation schedule** in Export TimelineJS. Its default date starts at local today; selected events can receive a temporary date, time and **Start**, **Do the action** or **Arrive** meaning. Relative and position events with recorded dates also export without enabling a schedule.

Scheduled dates are labeled presentation assumptions. Original dates/times and relative-placement notes remain visible; no durations or before/after timing are inferred. TimelineJS sorts chronologically, which can differ from narrative placement; equal timestamps retain selected order. Invalid settings prevent projection until corrected. Returning from presentation retains settings, while closing or refreshing clears them. The ResearchTools backup always exports the same frozen original snapshot without temporary settings, including when projecting a saved or historical revision. No new API, schema, permission, persistence model or public sharing is introduced. **TL-17 remains partial.**
